OUR SERVICES

Our healthcare services.

Support for everyday health needs, delivered with care and attention.

🫀

Cardiology

Clear guidance before treatments and specialist care for common heart and cardiovascular concerns.

  • High blood pressure (Hypertension)
  • Heart diseases
  • Chest pain evaluation
  • High cholesterol
  • Heart failure
🧠

Neurology

Clear guidance before treatments and specialist support for neurological concerns.

  • Headache and migraines
  • Stroke and evaluation
  • Nerve disorders
🦴

Orthopedics

Specialist care for bones, joints, muscles, and mobility concerns.

  • Bone fractures
  • Knee pain
  • Neck pain
  • Joint disorders
  • Shoulder problems
👶

Pediatrics

Healthcare and wellness support for infants and children.

  • Childhood infections
  • Diarrhoea and vomiting
  • Nutritional deficiencies
  • New born baby checkups
🩺

General Medicine

Primary care, routine checkups, and general consultations.

  • Fever and infections
  • Diabetes
  • High blood pressure
  • Cold, cough, and flu
🦷

Dental Care

Preventive dental care and treatments for common tooth and oral health concerns.

  • Tooth pain treatments
  • Dental fillings
  • Teeth cleaning and scaling
  • Tooth extraction
